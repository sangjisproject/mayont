Delete your Mayont account

Mayont by Wu Kuan Yi

You can delete your Mayont account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Mayont and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tick I understand this can't be undone, then tap Delete my account.

You need an internet connection. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

From the address you sign in with, email sangjis.project@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Mayont account. We'll confirm, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

We delete your account, profile, logo and signature, customers and site addresses, jobs, photos, notes, reports, customer signatures, PDFs, and the feedback and screenshots you sent us. Nothing is kept on our servers. Links to reports you shared before stop working.

What is not deleted

  • Subscriptions bought on Google Play continue until you cancel them in Google Play.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records.
  • Crash reports, which carry no personal details beyond a random id, expire within 90 days.
  • PDFs you already sent to customers stay with them.
