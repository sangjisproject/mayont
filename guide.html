准备Setup

只需做一次Do this once

01

登录Sign in

  1. 打开 Mayont，输入你的邮箱，点 发送登录邮件。
  2. 在这台手机上打开邮件，点邮件里的链接；或者把邮件里的 6 位验证码输进 App。
  3. 不需要密码。也可以点 使用 Google 账号继续。
  1. Open Mayont, enter your email and tap Send sign-in email.
  2. Open the email on this phone and tap the link, or type the 6-digit code from the email into the app.
  3. There's no password. You can also tap Continue with Google.
02

填好公司资料Your company details

  1. 点下方的 设置 → 个人资料，填你的名字、公司名称和电话。这些会印在每份报告的抬头。
  2. 报告编号前缀是公司简称，例如 CBA。报告会自动编号为 CBA-2610-0001，意思是 2026 年 10 月的第 1 份。
  3. 想换 App 的语言，在 设置 → 界面语言 选中文、English 或 Bahasa Melayu。
  1. Tap Settings → Profile and fill in your name, company name and phone. They're printed at the top of every report.
  2. The report number prefix is your company's short code, such as CBA. Reports are numbered CBA-2610-0001: the first report of October 2026.
  3. To change the app's language, go to Settings → App language and pick 中文, English or Bahasa Melayu.

每一单Every job

从到达现场到发出报告From arriving on site to sending the report

03

添加客户Add a customer

  1. 点 客户 → 新建客户。
  2. 填客户名字、电话和地址，按 保存。
  3. 右上角的通讯录图标可以直接从手机通讯录导入，不用再打字。
  1. Tap Customers → New customer.
  2. Enter the name, phone and address, then tap Save.
  3. The contacts icon at the top right imports someone straight from your phone's contacts.
小提示Tip 同一个客户可以有几个地址，例如住家和店面。每个地址的工单记录会分开保存。 One customer can have several addresses, like a home and a shop. Each address keeps its own job history.
04

开工单Start a job

  1. 到了现场，在 工单 页点 新建工单。
  2. 选客户、地址和工种，例如冷气保养、水管、电工。
  3. 点 开始工单，开工时间会自动记下来。
  1. On site, tap New job on the Jobs tab.
  2. Pick the customer, the address and the job type, such as aircon service, plumbing or electrical.
  3. Tap Start job. The start time is recorded for you.
小提示Tip 每个工种都自带一份检查清单。想改清单，去 设置 → 工种与检查清单。 Each job type comes with its own checklist. Change them in Settings → Job types & checklists.
05

拍照和口述Photos and voice notes

  1. 先选 维修前 或 维修后，再点 拍照，也可以从 相册 选。长按照片拖动可以调整顺序。
  2. 按住下面的麦克风 按住说话，说出发现的问题和你做了什么。放开后会变成文字，可以直接修改。
  3. 右上角可以选口述语言：中文、English 或 Bahasa Melayu。
  1. Choose Before or After, then tap Camera, or pick from the Gallery. Long-press a photo and drag it to reorder.
  2. Press and hold the mic (Hold to talk) and say what you found and what you did. Let go and it turns into text you can edit.
  3. Pick the speech language at the top right: 中文, English or Bahasa Melayu.
小提示Tip 说得越具体，报告越好：哪一台机、什么问题、换了什么、量到的温度或压力。 The more specific you are, the better the report: which unit, what was wrong, what you replaced, the temperature or pressure you measured.
06

勾清单、记零件Checklist and parts

  1. 做完一项就勾一项。清单上没有的，可以在 新的检查项目 自己加。
  2. 用了零件就点 添加零件，填数量和单价，合计（RM）会自动算好。
  3. 全部填好后，滑到最下面点 生成报告。
  1. Tick each item as you finish it. Add anything missing in the New checklist item box.
  2. Tap Add part for each part you used, with its quantity and unit price. The total in RM adds itself up.
  3. When everything is in, scroll to the bottom and tap Generate report.
07

检查 AI 报告Check the AI report

  1. 几秒钟后，AI 会把照片、口述、清单和零件整理成一份正式报告：发现问题、处理过程、使用零件、建议和下次保养日期。
  2. 顶部写着 AI 草稿——发送前请检查。每一栏都可以直接点进去修改。
  3. 客户看不懂中文？点右上角的语言按钮，把报告换成英文或马来文。
  1. In a few seconds the AI turns your photos, notes, checklist and parts into a proper report: problem found, work done, parts used, recommendations and the next service date.
  2. It's marked AI draft — check it before sending. Tap any section to edit it.
  3. Customer reads English or Malay? Tap the language button at the top right to switch the report's language.
没有网络时Offline App 会先用你的记录填好报告，你自己改一下就可以。 The app fills in the report from your notes instead. Tidy it up yourself.
08

客户签名Customer signature

  1. 在报告下方点 请客户签名，把手机交给客户。
  2. 客户在框里签名，可以填上自己的名字，然后按 保存。签错了按 清除 重来。
  3. 回到报告，会看到签名和签名时间。
  1. Below the report, tap Get customer signature and hand over the phone.
  2. The customer signs in the box, can type their name, and taps Save. Clear starts again.
  3. Back on the report you'll see the signature and when it was signed.
09

发送 PDFSend the PDF

  1. 点 发送 PDF。App 会提醒：发送后报告会锁定，不能再修改。按 发送。
  2. 在分享菜单里选 WhatsApp，发给客户就完成了。
  3. 也可以点 发送链接，给客户一个下载链接（1 小时内有效）。
  1. Tap Send PDF. The app warns that sending locks the report, so it can't be changed. Tap Send.
  2. Choose WhatsApp in the share menu and send it to your customer. Done.
  3. Or tap Send link to give the customer a download link. It works for 1 hour.
发出去后要改？Need a change after sending? 点 新版本 另开一版来改，旧版本会保留。分享 PDF 可以再发一次。 Tap New version and edit that; the old one is kept. Share PDF sends it again.

用熟之后会用到的Useful once you're up and running

10

查看以前的工单Past jobs

  1. 工单 页只显示今天的工单。以前的在右上角 历史工单 里，最新的在最上面。
  2. 想看某个地址做过的所有工单：点 客户，选一位客户，再点他的地址。
  3. 点进工单可以看当时的报告。
  1. The Jobs tab shows today's jobs. Earlier ones are under History at the top right, newest first.
  2. To see every job done at one address, tap Customers, pick a customer, then tap one of their addresses.
  3. Tap a job to open its report.
小提示Tip 回访前先看一眼，就知道上次换过什么零件、给过什么建议。 Check it before a return visit to see what you replaced and recommended last time.
11

免费版和专业版Free and Pro

  1. 免费版：每月 5 份报告，PDF 底部有一行小字「Made with Mayont」。剩几份可以在 设置 → 方案 看到。
  2. 专业版：报告不限份数，去掉水印，PDF 显示你的公司 Logo。
  3. 价格：月付 RM 19，年付 RM 199，终身买断 RM 399（只限前 500 名）。在 设置 → 升级 购买，通过 Google Play 付款。
  1. Free: 5 reports a month, with a small "Made with Mayont" line at the bottom of each PDF. Settings → Plan shows how many you have left.
  2. Pro: unlimited reports, no watermark, and your company logo on every PDF.
  3. Prices: RM 19 a month, RM 199 a year, or RM 399 once for life (first 500 buyers only). Buy it in Settings → Upgrade; payment goes through Google Play.
12

遇到问题Problems or ideas

  1. 点 设置 → 反馈与问题报告 → 新反馈。
  2. 选 问题 或 建议，写下发生了什么。可以附最多 3 张截图。
  3. 我们的回复会出现在同一个页面。
  1. Tap Settings → Feedback & bug reports → New feedback.
  2. Choose Bug or Suggestion and describe what happened. You can attach up to 3 screenshots.
  3. Our reply shows up on the same page.

没有信号也能用Works without signal

在机房、地下室没信号时，照样可以拍照、填清单和零件、让客户签名。资料先存在手机里，有网络时会自动同步。只有发送报告和发送反馈一定要联网；没网时生成的报告，会用你的记录填好，不经过 AI。

In a plant room or basement with no signal you can still take photos, fill in the checklist and parts, and get the signature. Everything is kept on the phone and syncs once you're back online. Only sending the report and sending feedback need the internet. A report generated offline is filled in from your notes rather than by the AI.
