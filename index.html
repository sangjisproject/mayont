Mayont

Job reports in minutes, on site.

An Android app by Wu Kuan Yi.

Mayont helps aircon, plumbing, electrical and appliance technicians turn a site visit into a professional report. Take before and after photos, dictate what you found, let AI draft the report, get the customer's signature on your phone, and send a PDF on WhatsApp. It works offline and syncs when you're back online.
