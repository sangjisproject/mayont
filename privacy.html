Mayont ("the app") is operated by Wu Kuan Yi, Johor Bahru, Malaysia ("we"). Contact: sangjis.project@gmail.com.

We don't collect your location, your contacts list, or your advertising ID. The app reads a contact only when you pick one to fill in a customer, and keeps only the name and number you save. Voice dictation uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (on Android, usually Google's speech service, under Google's terms). We never receive or store audio, only the text you keep.

How it's processed

  • Storage. Your data is stored on your phone and synced to our database and file storage, hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in Singapore. Only your account can read it: every table and file is isolated per user. Photos and PDFs are kept in private storage. A link you share with a customer works for one hour.
  • AI report drafting. When you tap Generate, the job's photos, notes, checklist and parts are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to draft the report text. Your customer's name, phone and address are not sent. Anthropic processes the data for us as a service provider and doesn't use it to train its models.
  • Crash reports. These go to Sentry with a random account id. We don't attach your email, customer data, screenshots or IP address.
  • Payments. Google Play handles payments. We receive the purchase token to confirm your plan. We never see your card details.

We don't sell your data or show ads. We share data only with the service providers above, who process it solely to run Mayont. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Your customers' data

You decide what customer information to record. You're responsible for having a reason to collect it, such as doing and documenting the job they asked for. We process it only on your instructions, to provide the app.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or as described on Delete your account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, customers, jobs, photos, signatures, reports, PDFs and feedback from our servers, and removes them from that phone.

Crash reports expire from Sentry within 90 days. Google Play keeps its own purchase records. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel it in Google Play first.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and, by our hosting providers, at rest. Database row-level security limits access to each account's own data.

Your rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, export reports as PDFs, and delete everything at any time. For any other request, such as access or correction under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, email sangjis.project@gmail.com.

Children

Mayont is a work tool for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, or under 18 where local law requires.

Changes

We'll post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
